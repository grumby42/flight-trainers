How to fly
TiltHold the phone sideways like a yoke. Tilt left/right = bank. Tilt top toward you = nose up (slower), away = nose down (faster). Tap LEVEL to re-zero. Keys↑/W nose down · ↓/S nose up · ←/→ or A/D roll (bank holds) · L level wings · F / R flaps down / up · Space slip · [ ] trim · C camera · B wing-flap (Zen) · P pause GamepadLeft stick = yoke · A slip · LB/RB flaps · Y camera · Start pause TrimTrim sets the speed the duck holds with the phone at your LEVEL position (or keys released). It starts trimmed for best glide (Vg). Tilt = temporary change; trim = new "hands-off" speed. If you're holding the phone tilted to keep a speed, trim it out. Flaps change the trimmed speed (more lift at the same attitude), so re-trim after each notch — or leave Auto-trim on. LandingFlaps only once the pond is made; full flaps on final at the "Tgt" speed; round out ~25 ft, hold it off ~10 ft, touch near stall speed (~44 kt in the Mallard with full flaps). Bread trail🍞 Coins mark a glide path with margin. Collect them = right place, right energy. The PATH readout tells you high/low. Green ringMinimap glide reach at best glide in the current configuration, shifted by wind. Target inside ring = you can make it. Flight-path markerThe magenta ⊖ is where you're actually going. Put it just short of the bullseye on final — that's your aim point. PhysicsPoint-mass 3-DOF: real lift/drag polar (CD0 + k·CL²), stall at CLmax, ISA air density by altitude, ground effect, flaps & slip drag, boundary-layer wind, gusts, thermals and ridge lift/sink. Earth gravity on every world (even Planet Ember) so numbers transfer.
