Twin ✈ Vmc
Conceptual teaching model

This is a concept demonstrator, not a flight simulator. It illustrates the relationships behind Vmc, the critical engine, asymmetric thrust, and the single-engine drag picture — with tuned, non-literal magnitudes.

Do not read exact V-speeds, climb rates, or Vmc values as real-aircraft numbers. For figures use the AFM; for procedures and tolerances use the ACS. Use this to explain and brief the concept, then confirm specifics in the real references.
